# Create Account

Create a Business Account (Live Environment)

1. Go to the DOKU Malaysia Dashboard Registration page on this [link](https://dashboard.doku.com/bo/register?country=MY).
2. Fill in your:
    - Country
    - Full Name
    - Business Name
    - Email
    - Phone Number
    - Password
3. Agree to the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy, then submit.
4. Enter the OTP sent to your email to verify your account.
5. You're all set! Your Business Account is created.

👉 Next, follow the guide to activate your account.


:::highlight purple 
<Icon icon="material-outline-info"/> **Important**
DOKU had 2 environments which is Production and Sandbox, so you can create these 2 types of account:
1. **Live Business Account**
This is your official merchant account to accept real payments once you're verified and activated. You’ll use it to manage transactions, settlements, and access all DOKU features in production.
2. **Sandbox Account**
Sandbox Account can be created [here](https://sandbox.doku.com/bo/sandbox-registration). This allows you to explore and try DOKU features risk-free, and simulate payment flows using test credentials.
:::
